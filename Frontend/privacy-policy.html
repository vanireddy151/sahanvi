Privacy Policy

At Sahanvi Handloom Sarees, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is protected with the highest standards of security and confidentiality. By accessing or using our services, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this policy.

We collect personal information only when necessary to provide our products and services efficiently and enhance your shopping experience. The information we may collect includes:

  • Full Name

  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Identification Proof, where required for verification purposes
  • Payment and transaction details
  • Any additional information voluntarily shared by you while placing an order or contacting us

All information is collected through fair and lawful means, with your knowledge and consent.

How We Use Your Information

The information collected from you may be used for the following purposes:

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Data Protection & Security

We take appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Your personal information remains secure with us and will not be shared with any third party without your explicit consent, except where required by law or necessary for order fulfillment, such as logistics and payment processing partners.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including legal, accounting, and operational requirements.

Once the information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymize it.

Your Rights & Choices

You are free to decline our request for certain personal information, understanding that this may limit our ability to provide some services to you.

By continuing to use our website and services, you consent to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Contact Us
