Return & Exchange Policy

At Sahanvi Saree, every saree is thoughtfully handcrafted and carefully inspected to ensure exceptional quality before it reaches you. We take immense pride in offering authentic craftsmanship and a seamless shopping experience. However, in the unlikely event of a concern with your order, we have outlined a transparent return and exchange policy for your convenience.

Please note that all return and exchange requests are subject to review and approval by the Sahanvi Saree team and will be processed in accordance with the policy stated below.

Eligibility for Return & Exchange

To qualify for a return or exchange, the following conditions must be met:

  • The product must be unused, unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition.
  • All original packaging, tags, labels, invoice copies, and barcode stickers must remain intact.
  • The return request must be initiated within 24 hours of delivery.
  • Items that have been altered, customized, or damaged after delivery will not be eligible for return or exchange.
  • Only one return or exchange request will be accepted per order.

Please note that products will not be considered defective for:

  • Minor colour variations caused by photography lighting or individual screen/display settings.
  • Variations inherent to handcrafted and handwoven products, which are a natural characteristic of artisanal craftsmanship.
  • Personal dislikes related to colour tone, texture, weave, design preference, or fabric feel.

Non-Returnable & Non-Refundable Products

The following categories are not eligible for return, exchange, or refund:

  • Sarees with blouse stitching, fall/pico, tassel work, blouse detachment, or any custom tailoring services.
  • Products purchased during special promotions, clearance sales, or limited-time offers, unless received in damaged condition.
  • Products returned without original packaging, tags, or accessories.
  • Items showing signs of use, perfume, stains, damage, or tampering.

Return & Exchange Process

  • Customers must notify our support team within 24 hours of receiving the order by email, along with the order number and issue details.
  • Clear photographs and an unboxing video of the product must be shared for verification purposes.
  • Our Quality Assurance team will review the request and confirm eligibility for return or exchange.
  • Once approved, the product must be securely packed and shipped using a reliable courier service with tracking facilities.
  • After the returned product is received and successfully inspected, the approved refund or exchange will be processed within 7 working days.

Shipping Responsibility & Liability

In cases where the return or exchange is requested for reasons not attributable to Sahanvi Saree, the complete return shipping charges shall be borne by the customer.

Customers are strongly advised to use reputed courier services that provide tracking and insurance coverage. Sahanvi Saree shall not be held responsible for any loss, delay, or damage that occurs during return transit. The responsibility for the safe return of the product rests entirely with the sender until it is received by our team.

Situations Where Refunds Will Not Be Applicable

Refunds or exchanges will not be processed under the following circumstances:

  • Incorrect or incomplete shipping address provided by the customer.
  • Failed delivery attempts by the courier partner.
  • Refusal to accept the package upon delivery.
  • Delay in reporting the issue beyond the specified timeline.
  • Return requests based solely on personal preference or expectation mismatch.
